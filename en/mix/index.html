How we combine

  • Flat grips (PION, KASKADA, RAILQ) stand on taller posts; the bowed ARC and FRAME on low posts, joined at the waist.
  • Round rods (OBRĄCZKA, OSIKA, RAIL, STAVE) pass through a ring, ferrule or saddle of the same diameter.
  • GRID’s SPAR stands on square posts with a square saddle.
  • T and L knobs and the square ORBQ and DISCQ use a stem locked against turning.
  • The fluted FIELD-M plate always matches the knob size and never sits under GRID lattice knobs.
  • Any lever fits any rose; the escutcheon follows the rose shape.
  • Coated finishes (MB, WH, NK) for the whole set; AB-R only on textured models.
