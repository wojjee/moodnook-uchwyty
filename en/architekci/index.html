System, specification, samples

One thread, endless compositions.

Every handle is a few pieces joined by the same M4 thread. Choose the form, the posts and the finish – and when you want a change years later, replace one piece, not the whole handle.

Grip or head

Always with a female M4 thread underneath.

Ring, ferrule or saddle

For round rods: a flat seat and an M4 passage.

Post or stem

M4 at both ends; square ones with an anti-rotation pin.

Washer

A Ø5 spigot in the post foot – it always centres. M4 screw from the back.
