Installation & drilling template

  1. MarkPrint the template at 100% and check the 100 mm bar with a ruler. Tape it to the front and mark the hole centres.
  2. DrillDrill Ø4.5 mm through, square to the front. Back the exit side with a board to avoid tear-out.
  3. WasherPlace the washer – its Ø5 spigot sits in the post’s foot counterbore; align a square one with the edge.
  4. PostHand-thread the post or stem into the grip. Square posts carry an anti-rotation pin.
  5. M4 screwFrom inside, drive the M4 screw (length = front thickness + 6–8 mm). Tighten gently, no impact driver.
  6. SwapAny module unscrews and swaps later – one M4 thread across the system.

Screw lengths and torque to be confirmed in the spec sheet.
