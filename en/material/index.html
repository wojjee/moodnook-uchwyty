CW724R

Material & finishes

Solid lead-free silicon brass CW724R (CuZn21Si3P, ≤ 0.10 % Pb). Turned and milled from bar, no zinc castings. The largest ORB, ORB-F and ORBQ knobs (sizes 34 and 55) have a hollowed body closed with a brass cap; all other parts are solid. You feel the weight in your hand.

Material data
AlloyCW724R · CuZn21Si3P (EN 12164 / EN 12165)
CompositionCu 75–77 % · Si 2.7–3.5 % · P 0.02–0.10 % · Pb ≤ 0.10 % · Zn balance
Density8.3 g/cm³
Bar strengthRm ≥ 500–670 MPa (depending on diameter)
Dezincificationresistant alloy (test per ISO 6509)
ComplianceRoHS; REACH – per the material supplier’s declaration
Recycling100 % recyclable by remelting

Finishes

satin brass SB

A soft, matte satin glow – the most versatile. Brushed along the part’s axis. Uncoated.

polished brass PB

High gloss and warm gold – the brightest brass. Mirror polished. Uncoated.

aged brass AB

A deeper, aged tone – from day one. A chemical patina on the brass. Uncoated.

aged relief AB-R

Bright fields and ridges, dark grooves: the patina is hand-relieved from the high points – only on models with lines, flutes or lattice. Uncoated.

matte black MB

A deep matte black. A coating over the brass – the colour stays.

warm white WH

A warm, creamy white. A coating over the brass – the colour stays.

brushed nickel NK

A cool brushed nickel. A coating over the brass – the colour stays.

SB · PB · AB – uncoated brass: develops a patina where it is touched. Brass with natural antibacterial properties.

Finishes with polished accents (SB.PB, AB.PB) – for OTOCZAK, PION, OBRĄCZKA, OSIKA and KASKADA. Insert bezels are always polished.

Inserts

DISC, DISCQ, DRUM, DIAL and STUD knobs and the DIAL-E and STUD-E dimmers carry an exchangeable insert in a polished bezel: smoked glass, travertine, amber or brass. Travertine and amber also as a cabochon.

Care

Uncoated brass: a soft dry cloth. Coated finishes: a damp cloth and mild soap, no abrasives.
